Definitions and sources. Revenue is ServiceTitan completed jobs by completion date; January to March 2026 actuals come from the 2026 budget workbook's own actuals row, April to July from ServiceTitan, matching the delivered pacing workbook. Unique inbound callers is the count of distinct inbound caller phone numbers per brand per month from the ServiceTitan call roll ups. Bookings come from the Campaign Summary through April 2026 and the created-date job reports for May to July. Google Ads buckets are classified with the audited rules: Brand, Competitor, Non-Brand, and Suppressed is Google's own "Total: Other search terms" line; visible buckets tie to Google's "Total: Search terms" line to the penny in all 111 brand-months. PPC lines and GLSA are never combined. A missing value is rendered as a gap, never as zero.
